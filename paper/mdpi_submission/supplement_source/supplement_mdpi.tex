\section{Detailed Multi-Capacity and Information-Removal Analyses}

\subsection{Locked final-stage evaluation protocol}

The final-stage evaluation was specified after earlier OULAD and UCI results were available and is therefore a post-development stress test rather than preregistered confirmation. Before these analyses were computed, the protocol fixed review capacities of 5\%, 10\%, 20\%, and 30\%; the attributes examined for residual predictability; two operationally comparable information groups; a full-control replay tolerance of $10^{-12}$; 5,000 primary and 2,000 sensitivity bootstrap replicates; and Bonferroni-percentile families over every evaluated stage and capacity. The preserved XuetangX analysis was excluded from demographic claims because the official data schema did not support them.

The multi-capacity analysis reused saved probabilities and did not refit outcome models. The residual-attribute analysis fitted 44 diagnostic models and retained 250,044 held-out predictions. The comparable-information-group analysis fitted 28 required gradient-boosting models and no new UCI 697 outcome models; its 12 new full-information controls reproduced the locked probabilities with maximum absolute error $1.11\times10^{-16}$. The final paired analysis evaluated 151 contrasts in 42 bootstrap tasks and independently recalculated all 2,869 reported interval rows from hashed draws. Internal phase identifiers are retained in the repository manifests solely for reproducibility.

\subsection{Interpretation boundaries}

The following boundaries apply throughout. Residual attribute predictability is not evidence that the early-warning model used a particular proxy. Operationally comparable information groups do not establish measurement equivalence across datasets. A family-wise interval that includes zero is inconclusive rather than proof of equivalence, and duplicate reporting views of the same paired comparison are not independent confirmations. No result constitutes a fairness certification, privacy guarantee, formal non-inferiority conclusion, or causal estimate of benefit to educators or students. OULAD, UCI 697, and UCI 320 remain separately identified.

\subsection{Predictor inventory}

\paragraph{Context predictors.}
Module, number of previous attempts, studied credits, and registration lead days.

\paragraph{Click/activity and assessment-process predictors.}
Total clicks; active days; mean, standard deviation, and maximum daily clicks; first and last activity day; clicks and active days in the last seven days and the preceding seven days; pre-start clicks and active days; inactivity days; log click trend; assessments submitted; assessment weight completed; late submissions; assessments expected; assessment weight expected; assessment submission ratio; and assessment weight-completion ratio. Structurally all-missing training columns at a landmark were omitted.

\paragraph{Full-policy background fields.}
Gender, region, highest education, Index of Multiple Deprivation band, age band, and disability.

\paragraph{Strict original tier.}
Module, previous attempts, studied credits, registration lead days, assessments submitted, assessment weight completed, late submissions, assessments expected, assessment weight expected, assessment submission ratio, and assessment weight-completion ratio.

\paragraph{Strict enriched tier.}
The enriched policy retained click-independent context and added only typed process features whose due date and submission availability were no later than the landmark. It excluded scores, click/activity telemetry, and future records. A separately typed alternative and an expanded leaf grid were retained as unsuccessful sensitivities.

\paragraph{UCI 697 policies.}
The full enrollment set comprised 24 fields. The legacy operational set retained six admissions/course fields. Direct-personal exclusion removed marital status, nationality, displacement, educational special-needs status, gender, age at enrollment, and international status. Family/financial exclusion removed parental qualifications and occupations, debtor and tuition status, and scholarship status. Targeted exclusion removed gender and special-needs status. Semester-one policies added the six first-semester curricular-unit fields; second-semester fields were prohibited.

\paragraph{UCI 320 policies.}
Full policies used all non-target fields available at baseline, with G1, or with G1+G2. Demographic/family exclusion removed sex, age, address, family size and parental status, parental education and occupation, and guardian. Sensitive/wellbeing exclusion removed romantic relationship, family relationship, weekday and weekend alcohol use, and health. The school-observable tier retained school, reason, study time, prior failures, school support, paid classes, activities, absences, and grades available at the stage. These are frozen operational group names rather than claims of intrinsic sensitivity or legality.
