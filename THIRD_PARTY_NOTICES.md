# Third-party datasets and software

## Datasets not redistributed

- Open University Learning Analytics Dataset (OULAD), UCI dataset 349: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/349/open+university+learning+analytics+dataset
- Predict Students' Dropout and Academic Success, UCI dataset 697: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/697/predict+students+dropout+and+academic+success
- Student Performance, UCI dataset 320: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/320/student+performance

The UCI pages identify these datasets as CC BY 4.0. This release does not redistribute their raw or processed row-level records.

The canonical XuetangX C05/v0.3.1 archive is not included. Its derived demographic material could not be independently connected to the official KDD Cup 2015 schema and was excluded from the article's demographic evidence.

## Software

The analysis uses Python, NumPy, pandas, SciPy, scikit-learn, joblib, and Matplotlib under their respective upstream licenses. Version pins are listed in `requirements.txt`.
